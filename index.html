The robot's rules

Each row is something the three sensors can see. Pick what the motors should do when it happens.

Filled circle: the sensor sees black tape. Empty circle: it sees the white floor.

Sense

Each sensor shines infrared light at the floor. Black tape soaks the light up, while the white floor bounces it back, so the sensor can tell tape from floor.

Decide

The robot's brain looks at which sensors see tape and finds the matching rule. A basic line follower needs nothing more, except a tiny memory of which side the line was last on.

Act

Each rule sets the speed of two motors. A robot steers by driving its wheels at different speeds: it turns toward the slower wheel.

Things to try

  • Choose the rule set "Left and right swapped (bug)" and watch the robot steer away from the line.
  • Choose "Stops when lost" and see how much the robot depends on its search rule.
  • Raise motor power to 100% and compare the lap time and the wobble with 35%. What do you gain and lose?
  • Switch both side rules to curves instead of sharp turns. Which tracks still work?
  • Make the sensor spacing very small, then very large. What changes?
  • Clear the tape and draw a figure eight. What should the robot do at the crossing?
